Support PetFinder

PetFinder helps lost pets get back home. Owners report the pet they lost, people who find a stray post it with a photo and location, and the app automatically finds possible matches.

The app is and will always be free for everyone. No one will ever pay to find their pet. Running it, however, has costs:

  • servers for reports and photos
  • AI photo comparison
  • maps

If you'd like to help, a small, completely optional donation keeps PetFinder running for everyone.
